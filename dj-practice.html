QUICK START

1. 📁 USB → choose the folder with your songs. 2. On each CDJ press BROWSE (above the screen), scroll the round rotary knob right of the screen and click it to open a folder / load a track (or just double-click a row). 3. WORKS LIKE A REAL CDJ

CUE when paused = sets the cue point where the track is now. Hold CUE on the cue point and it plays; let go and it jumps back. Press PLAY while holding CUE to keep playing. CUE while playing = jump back to the cue point and pause. The tempo slider works like on a CDJ: down = faster (+). With QUANTIZE on, cues, loops and jumps snap to the beat grid. MASTER TEMPO = change the tempo without changing the key (key lock). BEAT SYNC follows the CDJ set as MASTER. To learn beatmatching, keep SYNC off and watch the PHASE bar under the waveform.

BEAT GRID

BPM and the beat grid are worked out automatically when you load a track. The key is detected automatically too (it can be wrong sometimes). If the grid doesn't sit exactly on the kicks: tap GRID under the waveform, pause on a kick and press SET ▮, or move it with ◀ ▶ (Shift = bigger step). Use ÷2 / ×2 if the BPM is double or half what it should be. Your changes are saved for next time.

DIFFERENCES FROM THE REAL THING

The mixer has 2 channels (CDJ 1 → CH 1, CDJ 2 → CH 2). There's no mic, Bluetooth, booth output or SD card. The effects are close to the A9 sound, but not exact. Your hands will feel the difference on real hardware, but the flow, the logic and beatmatching are the same.
